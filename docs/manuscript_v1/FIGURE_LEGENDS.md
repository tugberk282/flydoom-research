# Figure legends V1

## Figure 1 — Descriptive BM structural comparison across datasets

Non-self directed density (A; logarithmic axis) and reciprocal directed fraction (B) for the MaleCNS, FAFB and BANC BM-induced subgraphs at minimum synapse thresholds 1, 3 and 5. These descriptive differences jointly reflect specimen, sex, reconstruction, annotation and source-pipeline effects and therefore do not isolate biological sex.

## Figure 2 — Side/count-matched sensitivity

Distributions from 1,000 unique FAFB subsets matched to the MaleCNS BM side composition and node count. Panels show density and reciprocity at thresholds 3 (A–B) and 5 (C–D). Blue lines denote MaleCNS BM and dashed lines the subset 99th percentile. Subsets share nodes and edges and are sensitivity draws, not independent biological replicates or a formal inferential null.

## Figure 3 — Within-dataset sensory-family enrichment

BM midrank percentile relative to eligible sensory families in MaleCNS (A; 26 controls) and FlyWire (B; 27 controls). Density and reciprocity are shown across thresholds; the dashed line is an exploratory 0.95 guide. The mixed pattern does not establish a BM-specific structural effect.

## Figure 4 — Shared sensory-family scale residual

Robust z scores for the MaleCNS/FAFB BM log-ratio relative to 52 shared sensory-family log-ratios, for density (A) and reciprocity (B). The shaded band spans −2 to 2. Threshold-5 reciprocity reaches z = 2.82 but remains below the frozen strong-residual criterion of z ≥ 3; it is retained as a candidate, not a confirmed result.

## Figure 5 — Evidence map

Separation of the development finding, proposed mechanistic chain, external tests and bounded conclusion. Side/count matching supports the structural contrast, whereas BM specificity and local-mechanism replication remain unestablished. The current result is a reproducible structural contrast plus a constrained hypothesis, not a confirmed sex-specific discovery.
