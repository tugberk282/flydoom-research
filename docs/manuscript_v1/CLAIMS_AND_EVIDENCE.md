## Prohibited conclusions

- “The male fly brain is superior.”
- “Male flies have stronger memory.”
- “The connectome learned the maze biologically.”
- “A sex difference was demonstrated.”
- “BANC disproved the mechanism.”
- “The threshold-5 residual is statistically significant.”
- “One thousand subsets are one thousand biological replicates.”
- “A new release of the same specimen is independent replication.”
- “Publication originality has been established.”

## Claim-state vocabulary

- **Finding:** directly measured by a verified output.
- **Development inference:** mechanism localized after opening a development outcome.
- **Sensitivity finding:** robustness or confound test within existing specimens.
- **Hypothesis:** justified candidate requiring new independent data.
- **Negative result:** a frozen gate or criterion was not met.
- **Data limitation:** the necessary sampling unit is unavailable.
