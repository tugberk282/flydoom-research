# Reconstruction-sensitivity addendum — 4 November 2026

## Status

This addendum supersedes the FAFB-source-dependent interpretation in Manuscript V1 while preserving V1 as a historical, hash-verifiable package. The authoritative evidence bundle for the updated claims is `outputs/manuscript-evidence-v2/`.

## What changed

1. The FlyWire temporal graph used by V1 was identified exactly as the **Buhmann no-threshold** product (318,984/318,984 directed pairs and all integer weights identical).
2. Buhmann and Princeton products differ strongly on the frozen FAFB BM node set and the full temporal interface. Princeton is not treated as ground truth; this is measurement sensitivity within the same specimen.
3. Replacing only the FAFB structural source with Princeton preserved the raw Male-greater-than-FAFB direction, but changed the shared-family residual decision from historical `mixed_residual` to `global_scale_sufficient`. The former threshold-5 reciprocity candidate fell from robust z 2.816 / percentile 1.0 to z −0.0777 / percentile 0.4717.
4. Female Real delay-8 retention increased from 0.434824 (Buhmann) to 0.539272 (Princeton), showing that absolute retention magnitude is reconstruction-sensitive.
5. Princeton-specific matched nulls also increased. The primary within-type-block degree-rewire contrast was only +0.029772, below the frozen +0.05 effect floor. The historical Buhmann contrast was +0.003494. Thus Real neuron-specific partner superiority is unsupported in both products at the prespecified threshold.

## Revised conclusion

The supported manuscript-level result is methodological and negative/mixed: reconstruction choice materially changes both structural residuals and temporal-effect magnitude, while the primary degree-matched-null test does not support a robust Real neuron-specific wiring advantage. The MaleCNS development mechanism remains a valid within-dataset posthoc localization for its original graph and cue, but it is not a reconstruction-invariant or externally confirmed biological mechanism.

No sex difference, population generalization, biological memory, or reconstruction accuracy ranking is established. No new confirmation stage is opened.
